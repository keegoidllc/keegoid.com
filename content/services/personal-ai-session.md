## A good fit when

You are curious about AI but want help connecting it to something concrete. You may have tried a few prompts, found the results uneven, or simply want a clearer place to start.

- Preparation for your assessment.
- A focused 60-minute virtual personal AI assessment, including a demonstration of useful AI opportunities.
- A takeaway guide you can refer to afterward.

The initial assessment is $100 USD plus applicable tax, prepaid. You can stop after the assessment and use the guide to try the approach yourself.

## Choose a starting point

Think of a task you repeat or a project you would like help moving forward. Share that goal when you get in touch, and use it as the starting point for the assessment.
