## Everyday help, one monthly price

Keegoid LLC provides remote help with everyday Windows, Mac and Linux computer issues, questions, and routine maintenance. Covered support has no hourly fee.

## Is it right for your computer?

This service covers remote help with everyday Windows, Mac and Linux computer issues, questions, and routine maintenance. Typical support hours are Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–8 p.m. Pacific Time. On-site visits and physical repairs are outside its scope. Other work requires a separate quote and your approval.

Guilt-Free IT is an existing subscription offering, separate from the AI consulting services.
