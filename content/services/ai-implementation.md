## A good fit when

You have a defined use for AI and are ready to discuss implementing it. The project might center on a repeated workflow or a practical use identified in a [business AI assessment](/services/business-ai-assessment/).

## Start with scope

Each implementation is scoped individually. Typical projects fall between **$2,500 and $5,000 USD**; the project quote determines the actual scope and price.

Get in touch with the workflow you have in mind. The first step is a conversation about the work and a project quote.

## Payment structure

Payment is **50% deposit and 50% at acceptance**. The [scope and payment section](/terms/ai-consulting/#ai-implementation) summarizes this structure.
