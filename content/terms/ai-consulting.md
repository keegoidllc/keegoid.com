## Personal AI session

**$250 USD plus applicable tax, prepaid.** Includes a 60-minute virtual personal AI session, preparation, and a takeaway guide.

[Explore the personal AI session](/services/personal-ai-session/).

## Business AI assessment

**$250 USD plus applicable tax, prepaid.** Includes intake, a 90-minute virtual assessment of one costly or repetitive business process, a demonstration of relevant AI potential, and a short plan with a practical recommendation and an honest benefit estimate. Benefits are estimates; measurable savings are not guaranteed.

The initial assessment is a separate purchase. The implementation follow-up below is optional.

[Explore the business AI assessment](/services/business-ai-assessment/).

## Cancellation and rescheduling

For personal AI sessions and business AI assessments, contact [support@keegoid.com](mailto:support@keegoid.com?subject=AI%20session%20cancellation%20or%20rescheduling) to request cancellation or rescheduling. Cancel before the scheduled session or assessment begins for a full refund. If Keegoid cancels and you do not agree to a replacement time, your payment is refunded.

## AI implementation

### Business implementation follow-up

**$500 USD plus applicable tax, purchased separately.** This optional follow-up after a business assessment implements one agreed small workflow and teaches you how to use it. Agree the written scope, deliverables, acceptance criteria, timing, and cancellation/refund terms before purchase. Acceptance is confirmed in writing against those criteria. Larger integrations and work outside the agreed scope require a separate quote and your approval.

[Explore the optional business follow-up](/services/business-ai-assessment/#optional-implementation).

### Larger implementation projects

Each implementation is individually scoped. Typical projects are **$2,500–$5,000 USD**; the individual project quote determines scope and price. Payment is **50% deposit and 50% at acceptance**.

Your project quote states the deliverables and acceptance criteria. Acceptance occurs when you confirm in writing that those criteria have been met. Work outside the agreed scope requires a separate quote and your approval.

Your quote states the cancellation terms and treatment of the deposit before you approve the project and pay the deposit.

[Discuss an implementation](/services/ai-implementation/).

## Earlier purchases

These offers apply to new purchases. A purchase made under an earlier offer keeps its agreed scope and terms.
