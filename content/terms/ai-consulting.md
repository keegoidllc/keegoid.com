## Personal AI session

**$250 USD plus applicable tax, prepaid.** Includes a 60-minute virtual personal AI session, preparation, and a takeaway guide.

[Explore the personal AI session](/services/personal-ai-session/).

## Business AI assessment

**$750 USD plus applicable tax, prepaid.** Includes intake, a 90-minute virtual business AI assessment, and a short plan.

[Explore the business AI assessment](/services/business-ai-assessment/).

## Cancellation and rescheduling

For personal AI sessions and business AI assessments, contact [support@keegoid.com](mailto:support@keegoid.com?subject=AI%20session%20cancellation%20or%20rescheduling) to request cancellation or rescheduling. Cancel before the scheduled session or assessment begins for a full refund. If Keegoid cancels and you do not agree to a replacement time, your payment is refunded.

## AI implementation

Each implementation is individually scoped. Typical projects are **$2,500–$5,000 USD**; the individual project quote determines scope and price. Payment is **50% deposit and 50% at acceptance**.

Your project quote states the deliverables and acceptance criteria. Acceptance occurs when you confirm in writing that those criteria have been met. Work outside the agreed scope requires a separate quote and your approval.

Your quote states the cancellation terms and treatment of the deposit before you approve the project and pay the deposit.
