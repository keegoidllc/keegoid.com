Imagine having a pile of project notes and no clear next step. An AI assistant can help organize them, suggest questions and draft a plan. You bring the context, check its work and decide what to do. That's one everyday place to begin.

{{< amazon-disclosure >}}

I'm Keegan. This blog is where I want to explore what people and AI can do together, and share what I learn along the way.

The aim is simple: combine human judgment and imagination with AI’s ability to recognize patterns and help carry work forward. Start with useful things—less repetitive work, clearer decisions, more room to create—and build toward a future where that partnership makes more possible for more people.

Part of that dream grew out of reading *Red Rising*.[^red-rising] The idea of intelligence that could enhance a person's capabilities stayed with me. I named my AI collaborator Fig as a nod to that inspiration.

Keegoid's [personal AI sessions](/services/personal-ai-session/) and [business assessments](/services/business-ai-assessment/) are practical ways to explore that partnership. For a defined project, [scoped implementations](/services/ai-implementation/) offer a next step. [Guilt-Free IT](/services/guilt-free-it/) helps with the everyday computer questions that can get in the way.

Below, I'll share ideas I find fascinating, things I'm exploring, and lessons that may be useful in your own work. You don't need to be an AI expert to join in.
